About

The artist

A contemporary figurative painter with roots in South Asian art and a background in tech.

Ramya Shenoy is a figurative painter. She paints portraits of women in a bold, colorful style influenced by South Asian art.

She came to painting after a career in technology and holds a master's degree in computer science. She lives and works in the United States, and has exhibited in galleries in Chicago and New York.

Artist statement

I paint portraits of women, using bright, saturated color. Recurring symbols such as fruit, a crown, or a butterfly turn up in the work, drawn from the imagery and stories I grew up around.
