About

The artist

A contemporary figurative painter working at the edge of symbolism, cultural memory, and feminine agency.

Ramya Shenoy is a contemporary figurative painter whose work explores feminine defiance, cultural memory, and quiet transformation. Drawing on both traditional South Asian visual language and modern sensibilities, her portraits seek to capture the subtle power and layered emotional landscape of her subjects.

With a background in both technology and the arts, Ramya fuses structure with symbolism, boldness with tenderness, and narrative with nuance. Color becomes a language of mood; gesture becomes a claim to presence.

Artist statement

My paintings begin with women who refuse to be simplified. Some confront the viewer directly. Others turn inward, holding silence like a form of power. Across the work, I am interested in the threshold between softness and resistance — the private labor of becoming, and the public act of being seen on one’s own terms.

Symbolism — fruit, crown, butterfly, vine — enters as ritual rather than decoration. These motifs carry cultural memory while remaining open enough for the viewer to find their own reading. I paint to make space for women who look back, withhold, emerge, and choose.
