Chrysalis

A series of intimate portraits exploring the quiet strength of transformation. Each painting reflects a moment of introspection and emergence — capturing the duality of softness and resilience in womanhood. Chrysalis speaks to the stages of becoming, of unfolding power in stillness and in resistance.
