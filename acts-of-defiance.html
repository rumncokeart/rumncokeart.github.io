Acts of Defiance

Acts of Defiance is about women who look back. They meet your gaze, hold their ground, and refuse to be simplified or ignored. It’s less about how women are supposed to be seen, and more about how they choose to be seen — whether that’s mid-bite of an apple or wearing a crown.
